## 1. Overview

The **EWU Academic Management Portal** is your all-in-one academic platform at East West University. As a student, you can:

- Plan your upcoming semester courses in the **Pre-Advising** phase.
- Register for official course sections during **Final Advising** windows.
- View your full **academic transcript** (grades and CGPA).
- Monitor your **financial ledger** and installment status.
- Submit **override or exception requests** to your Faculty Advisor.
- Communicate directly with your advisor through the **messaging** feature.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Account Activation

> **Important**: First-time students must activate their account before logging in for the first time.

**Steps to activate your account:**

1. Open the portal in your web browser.
2. On the login page, click the **"Activate Your ID First"** link.
3. Enter your **Student ID** (e.g., `2023-2-60-010`) or your **university email address** (e.g., `2023-2-60-010@std.ewubd.edu`).
4. Click **"Send Verification Code"**.
5. Check your university email inbox for a **6-digit OTP code** (valid for 10 minutes).
6. Enter the OTP code on the verification page and click **"Verify Code"**.
7. Set a new password:
   - Must be **6 to 12 characters** long.
   - Enter the password twice to confirm.
8. Click **"Activate Account"**. You can now log in.

> **Note**: If you do not receive the email, check your spam/junk folder. The code expires after 10 minutes — you may request a new one by going back and repeating the process.

---

### 2.2 Logging In

**Method 1 — Standard Login:**

1. Go to the portal login page.
2. Enter your **Student ID** (e.g., `2023-2-60-010`) or your **university email** in the "Email / Student ID" field.
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click **"Sign In"**.
5. You will be automatically redirected to your **Student Dashboard**.

**Method 2 — Google Sign-In (One-Click):**

1. On the login page, click the **"Continue with Google"** button.
2. Select your **university Google/Gmail account**.
3. If your Google account is already linked to your Student ID, you will be signed in immediately.
4. **First-time Google link**: If it is your first time using Google Sign-In, you will be prompted to enter your Student ID and password once to permanently link your Google account. After that, you can use 1-click Google Sign-In forever.

---

### 2.3 Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Enter your **Student ID** or **university email address**.
3. Click **"Send Reset Code"**.
4. A **6-digit reset code** will be emailed to your registered address.
5. Enter the code on the next screen and click **"Verify"**.
6. Set and confirm your **new password** (6–12 characters).
7. Click **"Reset Password"** — you can now log in with your new password.

## 4. Pre-Advising Planner

The **Pre-Advising** phase runs before official registration opens. It allows you to build a preferred course roadmap for the upcoming semester so the university can plan section capacities.

### How to use the Pre-Advising Planner:

1. From the sidebar, click **"Pre-Advising"**.
2. You will see a list of **eligible courses** based on:
   - Your completed credits and prerequisites.
   - Your department-specific curriculum.
   - Credit limit restrictions.
3. Browse available courses. Each card shows:
   - Course code and title
   - Number of credits
   - Prerequisites required
4. Click the **"+ Add"** button on a course to add it to your Pre-Advising Plan.
5. You may select **up to 6 courses** (maximum of **21 credits**).
6. To remove a course, click the **"Remove"** button on the course card.
7. Click **"Save Plan"** to submit your pre-advising course selection.

> **Note**: Pre-advising does NOT register you for a section — it is a planning step only. Actual seat registration happens during Final Advising.

### Pre-Advising Rules:
- You cannot add a course if its prerequisites are not completed.
- You cannot exceed the 6-course / 21-credit limit.
- Non-departmental courses are limited to a maximum of 3 per your degree plan.
- You can update your plan any time before the pre-advising window closes.

## 5. Final Advising & Registration

The **Final Advising** tab opens only during your **credit-bracket registration window**, which is a scheduled time slot assigned by the administration based on your completed credits.

### Understanding Registration Windows:
Students with more completed credits get earlier registration windows (priority access). Your window will open and close at specific date and times configured by the Admin.

### How to Register for Courses:

1. From the sidebar, click **"Advising"** (or navigate to `/advising`).
2. You will see the available **section offerings** for the upcoming semester.
3. For each section, the system displays:
   - Course code & title
   - Section number
   - Credits
   - Schedule (e.g., `MW 10:10 AM – 11:40 AM`)
   - Room
   - Seats available / total capacity
4. Click **"Register"** next to a section to enroll.
5. The system will **automatically validate**:
   - ✅ Prerequisites completed
   - ✅ Schedule conflict (no time overlap)
   - ✅ Seat availability
   - ✅ Financial clearance (no outstanding balance hold)
   - ✅ Credit limit (you cannot exceed your allowed credits)
   - ✅ Department seat reservations
6. If a **theory course** has a linked **lab section**, both are registered simultaneously as one atomic transaction.
7. To **drop** a registered section, click **"Drop"** next to the section.

### Registration Status Messages:
| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Registered | Successfully enrolled in section |
| ❌ Prerequisites not met | You must complete required courses first |
| ❌ Schedule conflict | Another registered course overlaps this time slot |
| ❌ Section full | No seats remaining — consider submitting an override request |
| ❌ Financial hold | Outstanding balance must be cleared first |
| ❌ Credit limit exceeded | Reduce total registered credits to proceed |
| ⏳ Window not open | Your credit bracket's registration time has not started yet |

Your **Transcript** tab displays a complete history of all your grades across all semesters.

### What is shown:

|---|---|

| A | 3.75 |
| A- | 3.50 |
| B+ | 3.25 |
| B | 3.00 |

## 7. Financial Ledger

The **Financial** tab provides full transparency into your tuition account.

### What is shown:

**Ledger Entries:**
- A chronological list of all tuition charges and payments.
- Each entry shows: description, amount, date, and status (Paid / Unpaid).

**Installment Plan:**
- Breakdown of your semester tuition into installments.
- Each installment shows: installment number, due date, amount, and payment status.

**Financial Hold Indicator:**
- If you have an **outstanding balance** (unpaid fees), a **Financial Hold** flag will be shown.
- A financial hold **prevents you from registering** for new sections during advising.
- Contact the university finance office to clear holds.

## 8. Override & Exception Requests

If you cannot register for a section due to it being full, department-restricted, or require a section change, you can submit an **Override Request** to your Faculty Advisor.

### Types of Requests:
| Request Type | When to Use |
|---|---|
| **Add Course** | Course is eligible but section is full or restricted |
| **Change Section** | You are registered in one section but want to move to another |

### How to Submit an Override Request:

1. In the **Advising** tab, locate the section you need.
2. Click **"Request Override"** (available when normal registration fails).
3. Select the **request type** (Add Course / Change Section).
4. Provide a **reason/comment** explaining your request.
5. Click **"Submit Request"**.
6. Your request is immediately forwarded to your assigned Faculty Advisor via email and the portal.

### Tracking Your Request:

- View all submitted requests in the **Advising** tab under **"My Requests"**.
- Statuses:
  - 🟡 **Pending** — Awaiting your advisor's decision
  - ✅ **Approved** — Your advisor approved the request
  - ❌ **Rejected** — Your advisor rejected the request with a note
- You will receive a **portal notification** and an **email** when your advisor acts on your request.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I can't log in even though I entered the correct ID and password.**  
A: Make sure your account is **activated**. New students must complete the activation process before logging in. Click "Activate Your ID First" on the login page.

**Q: Why can't I register for a course I need?**  
A: Common reasons include: prerequisites not met, section is full, financial hold, schedule conflict, or your registration window is not yet open. Check the error message shown when you attempt to register.

**Q: My registration window shows "Not Open Yet." What do I do?**  
A: Your credit-bracket window has not started yet. The Admin schedules windows by completed credits — students with more credits get earlier access. Check the dates shown on the Advising page.

**Q: Can I register for a lab without the theory course?**  
A: No. Theory and lab sections are paired. When you register for a theory course that has a linked lab, the system automatically registers both simultaneously. You cannot enroll in one without the other.

**Q: My override request was rejected. Can I resubmit?**  
A: Yes. You may resubmit with additional justification. Contact your advisor directly for guidance.

**Q: How do I clear a financial hold?**  
A: Visit or contact the East West University Finance Office. Once your balance is cleared, the hold will be lifted and you can register.

**Q: How do I change my password?**  
A: Log out, then use the **"Forgot Password?"** link on the login page to reset it via email OTP.
