## 1. Overview

The **EWU Academic Management Portal — Faculty Interface** is your central workspace for managing academic responsibilities at East West University. As a **Faculty Member / Academic Advisor**, you can:

- View and manage your complete **advisee roster**.
- Inspect individual **student academic profiles**, transcripts, and registration history.
- **Approve or reject** student override/exception requests for course additions and section swaps.
- Record daily **attendance** for all sections you teach.
- Enter **course marks** for each grade component (Midterm, Final, Quizzes, Attendance, etc.) and publish final grades automatically.
- Upload **course materials** (slides, notes, assignments, lab manuals) to your sections.
- Post **section announcements** for enrolled students.
- **Communicate directly** with students through the portal messaging system.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Logging In

Faculty accounts are **pre-activated** by the system — no manual activation is required.

**Standard Login:**
1. Go to the portal login page.
2. Enter your **Faculty Email** (e.g., `faculty@ewubd.edu`) or **Faculty ID** in the identifier field.
3. Enter your **password**.
4. Click **"Sign In"**.
5. You will be automatically redirected to your **Faculty Dashboard**.

**Google Sign-In (One-Click):**
1. Click **"Continue with Google"** on the login page.
2. Select your faculty Google/Gmail account.
3. If your Google account is already linked, you will be signed in immediately.
4. **First-time Google link**: Enter your Faculty ID and password once to permanently link your Google account.

---

### 2.2 First-Time Access

If you are accessing the portal for the first time with a standard password provided by the administration:
1. Log in with your provided credentials.
2. Navigate to your **Profile** to update your personal information.
3. To change your password, log out and use **"Forgot Password?"** to reset it via email OTP.

---

### 2.3 Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**.
2. Enter your **Faculty Email** or **Faculty ID**.
3. Click **"Send Reset Code"**.
4. A **6-digit reset code** will be sent to your registered email.
5. Enter the code on the verification screen.
6. Set and confirm your **new password** (6–12 characters).
7. Click **"Reset Password"**.

## 4. Managing Your Advisees

The **Advisees** tab gives you a full roster of all students assigned to you as their academic advisor.

### Viewing the Advisee Roster:

1. Click **"Advisees"** in the sidebar.
2. A table lists all your advisees with:
   - Student ID
   - Student Name
   - Department
   - Completed Credits
   - CGPA
   - Advising Status (Not Started / Planned / Approved)
   - Financial Status (Cleared / Hold)

### Viewing a Student's Full Profile:

1. Click the **student's name** or the **"View Profile"** button next to any student.
2. You will see the student's complete academic profile including:
   - **Personal Details**: Name, ID, department, contact information.
   - **Academic Standing**: CGPA, completed credits, credit limit.
   - **Current Registrations**: Courses and sections the student is currently enrolled in.
   - **Pre-Advising Plan**: Courses the student selected during the pre-advising phase.
   - **Academic Transcript**: Full grade history across all semesters.
   - **Advising Request History**: All override/exception requests the student has ever submitted.
   - **Financial Summary**: Outstanding balance and installment status.

### Organized History Timeline:
- The student profile page includes a **"History Timeline"** tab showing a chronological view of all the student's advising requests, approvals, rejections, and registrations — giving you full context for decision-making.

## 5. Override & Advising Request Center

The **Requests** tab is where you review and resolve student requests for course additions and section swaps.

### Understanding Request Types:
| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Add Course** | Student wants to join a full or restricted section |
| **Change Section** | Student wants to switch from one section to another |

### Reviewing Requests:

1. Click **"Requests"** in the sidebar.
2. All **pending requests** are listed with:
   - Student Name and ID
   - Course Code and Section requested
   - Request Type (Add Course / Change Section)
   - Student's comment / reason
   - Date and time submitted
3. Click **"View"** on any request to see the student's full context before deciding.

### Approving a Request:

1. Open the request.
2. Read the student's reason carefully.
3. (Optional) Enter a **Faculty Note** — a response or explanation for the student.
4. Click **"✅ Approve"**.
5. The student receives an **instant notification** (in-portal + email) that their request was approved.
6. The system automatically updates the student's registration if the section has capacity.

### Rejecting a Request:

1. Open the request.
2. Enter a **Faculty Note** explaining the reason for rejection (strongly recommended for student clarity).
3. Click **"❌ Reject"**.
4. The student is notified immediately via portal notification and email.

### Batch Processing:
- Multiple pending requests can be reviewed and resolved sequentially for efficiency.

> **Email Notifications**: You receive an email whenever a student submits a new request. The email contains a direct link to the student's History Timeline for immediate review.

## 6. Attendance Management

The **Attendance** feature allows you to log daily attendance for each of your assigned teaching sections.

### Marking Attendance:

1. Click **"Attendance"** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **section** you want to mark attendance for from the dropdown.
3. Select the **date** (defaults to today).
4. You will see a roster of all enrolled students.
5. For each student, set their attendance status:
   - ✅ **Present**
   - ❌ **Absent**
   - ⏰ **Late**
6. Click **"Save Attendance"** to record the data.

### Viewing Attendance Reports:

- After saving, you can review the attendance history per section.
- The system tracks total attendance counts per student.
- Students with **low attendance** are flagged automatically, allowing you to monitor academic risk.

### Editing Past Attendance:

- Select a past date from the date picker.
- The previous attendance records will load.
- Make changes and click **"Save Attendance"** to update.

## 7. Grade Entry & Marksheets

The **Grades** feature allows you to enter student marks for each assessment component and publish final grades automatically.

### Step 1 — Configure Grading Scheme

1. Click **"Grades"** in the sidebar.
2. Select a **section** from the dropdown.
3. If no grading scheme exists for this section, set up the **component weights**:
   - Example: `Attendance: 10%`, `Quizzes: 20%`, `Midterm: 30%`, `Final: 40%`
   - Total must equal **100%**.
4. Click **"Save Grading Scheme"**.

### Step 2 — Enter Marks

1. Once the scheme is set, the student marksheet appears.
2. For each student, enter their raw marks for each component.
   - Example: Midterm score `/30`, Attendance `/10`, etc.
3. The system **automatically calculates**:
   - Total weighted marks
   - Percentage
   - Letter grade (`A+`, `A`, `B+`, … `F`)
   - Grade point (0.00 – 4.00)

### Grade Letter Mapping:
| Grade | Points | Percentage (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | 4.00 | 90% and above |
| A | 3.75 | 85–89% |
| A- | 3.50 | 80–84% |
| B+ | 3.25 | 75–79% |
| B | 3.00 | 70–74% |
| B- | 2.75 | 65–69% |
| C+ | 2.50 | 60–64% |
| C | 2.25 | 55–59% |
| D | 2.00 | 50–54% |
| F | 0.00 | Below 50% |

### Step 3 — Save & Publish Grades

1. After entering all marks, click **"Save Marks"** to save as a draft.
2. Review the auto-calculated grades carefully.
3. When ready, click **"Publish Grades"** to make grades visible to students.
4. Once published:
   - Student's academic transcript is updated.
   - Student's CGPA is recalculated.
   - Completed credits are updated if they passed.

> **Warning**: Published grades update student CGPA and transcript. Ensure all marks are correct before publishing.

## 8. Course Material Publishing

You can upload and organize course materials directly in the portal, making them accessible to all enrolled students in a section.

### Uploading Course Materials:

1. Click **"Materials"** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **section** you want to upload for.
3. Click **"Upload Material"**.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Title**: A descriptive name (e.g., `Week 3 - Lecture Slides`)
   - **File Type**: Select the category (`Slide`, `Note`, `Assignment`, `Lab`)
   - **File**: Upload the file from your device.
5. Click **"Upload"**.

### Managing Uploaded Materials:

- All uploaded materials are listed per section.
- Students enrolled in that section can download them from their portal.
- You can delete materials that are no longer needed.

### Supported File Types:
- PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, and common document formats.

## 9. Course Announcements

Post important notices directly to the students enrolled in a specific section.

### Posting a Section Announcement:

1. Click **"Materials"** or the section view in the sidebar.
2. Navigate to the **"Announcements"** sub-tab within a section.
3. Click **"New Announcement"**.
4. Enter a **Title** and the announcement **Content**.
5. Click **"Post Announcement"**.
6. All students enrolled in that section will see the announcement on their dashboard.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: A student submitted a request but I can't find it.**  
A: Check the **"Requests"** tab. Filter by **"Pending"** status. If still not visible, ensure you are the student's assigned Faculty Advisor.

**Q: Can I edit grades after publishing?**  
A: Contact the system administrator. Once grades are published, editing requires admin-level permissions to ensure grade integrity.

**Q: Can I assign myself as an advisor to a student?**  
A: Advisor assignments are managed by the **Admin**. Contact administration if a student's advisor assignment needs to be changed.

**Q: I cannot see a section in my attendance or grade pages.**  
A: Sections must be assigned to you as the faculty instructor by the Admin in the course catalog. Contact the Admin to ensure your sections are correctly configured.

**Q: How do I mark a student's attendance retroactively?**  
A: Open the Attendance tab, select the section, and change the date to the past date you want to correct. Update the records and save.

**Q: A student says they submitted a request but I didn't receive an email.**  
A: Check your spam/junk folder. You can also check the **Requests** tab directly in the portal, which is the most reliable way to view all pending requests.
